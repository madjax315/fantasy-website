 1.2: Keeper (5 total years-3 players-3 year max contract)
 
Before each season you will have the option to retain 3 players from your previous season's roster. (This does not mean you have to keep 3 players; you have the option to retain 1, 2, 3 or none). Each player you keep will cost their draft value from the season drafted. Example, if you drafted said player in the 4th round, this will cost you a 4th round draft pick to retain said player. This will hold true for every player drafted in their respective rounds. For any player that goes undrafted, they will hold the value of an 8th round draft pick. Their value will carry over for every season you retain them. 

A player may be kept under a 1, 2, or 3 year “contract”. The player must be kept for the entire extent of the contract (from keeper declaration day to final playoff game is one year). Players who are under contract can not be dropped, but they CAN be traded, so long as the receiving party does not drop the player and the keeper maximum rules are violated as a result of the trade. The receiving manager inherits the draft round restrictions of the traded players contract. 

Once a “contract” has been fulfilled the player will return to the draft pool.

You have a maximum of five (5) total “contract” years to allocate to your keepers. Example: you cannot have two 3-year contracts, because that would be 6 total years.
 
If you would like to keep two players who were drafted in the same round, through trades or waiver pick ups, you must give up that round and the previous rounds picks. For example, if you want to keep two players drafted in the 6th round, you must give up your 6th and 5th round pick.
 
Players drafted in the first, second, and third round will NOT be eligible to be retained or kept. 

Keepers will be announced to the league publicly. The retaining deadline will be announced every year by the commissioner. 

If a selected keeper is injured before their first game, an owner ay drop that keeper.
 
1.3: Trading
 
-	Owners will have the option to exchange players and draft picks during the trade window (draft picks can only be traded from the date of keeper declaration to the start of the draft). As long as both parties come to an agreement with no foul play the trade will be approved. 
-	(Expedited trades are eligible upon a league vote). 
-	The same trade can only be proposed once per week. Jaz.
 
1.3.1: Trade Collusion/Self-Destruction/Tanking
 
o	All trades will be reviewed by the commissioners to make sure no foul play is occurring. If any owners are proven of accepting bribes/offering bribes to enhance their/another owners team/ tanking or self-destructing their team which results in jeopardizing the integrity of the league, under verifiable evidence (i.e. text messages, emails, league data, etc.), they will be susceptible to punishment. 
The punishments will go as followed: 
o	 1st Offense -- Remove the ability to trade for that season

o	2nd Offense -- Loss of ability to keep Dynasty Players for 1 season

o	3rd Offense -- Loss of 1st round draft pick…. (other owners vote on said pick for those accused of collusion). Possible Removal / Fine 
 
o	All trades that are found guilty of collusion/tanking/ self-destruction, will be reversed/denied. If the trade had affected the outcome of previous games, the results of those games will be subject to revision upon a league vote.  
o	Commissioners can reverse and deny trades at anytime if they feel it is necessary to keep the integrity of the league. League can veto commissioner with 100% vote.
o	Offenses will stay on your record for 2 seasons before being expunged. 


1.3.2: Trade Deadline
 
o	The trading market will open right after the Keeper deadline has passed and everyone has announced their Keeper Players.
o	The trading deadline will be determined by the commissioner each season (or as determined by application.)

(Inactivity Period: Trade Deadline – 4 months after the Superbowl)
 
  
1.4: Waiver Wire
-	The waiver wire order, during the season, will be based on the outcome from the first week’s results, and every week thereafter. The order will only change when owners make waiver claims and will be sent to the bottom of the order if said claim is successful.

-	The waiver wire order is initially set after the draft and it is to be the reverse order of the draft order for that year.


Section 2: Ownership Expectations
 
2.1: League Fee
 
-	Upon election the commissioner shall set a date for league payment deadline. The owner is expected to pay this fee by the deadline, or they will be subject to losing ownership of their team. The league fee breakdown goes as follows:

●	$95 towards the grand prize.
●	$5 towards league expenses i.e. draft board, trophy etc...


-	The league fee could be subject to change each season based on league expenses. The basis of the league fee will be voted on as a whole to change the total prize money received by the winners.

 
2.3: Removal
 
-	The owners can vote to remove any owner from the league at any time. As long as they feel this owner is ruining the integrity of the league and they can provide acceptable evidence (This is up to commissioner interpretation)(must be unanimous vote for removal). 
-	If any owner is removed it will fall on the Commissioners to replace said owner. If an owner is removed before the start of the fantasy season they will receive a league fee refund. If it accrues during the fantasy season, they will not. The fantasy season starts once the league draft is completed.  
-	If an owner is removed in the middle of the fantasy season, it will fall on the Commissioner to manage said team until a new owner is found. 
-	No roster transactions will be allowed to occur with this team until a new owner is found. 
-	The Co-Commissioner shall take over for weeks when conflicting schedules may emerge.

2.4: Owner Replacement

-	If an owner is voted out of the league their team will finish the season under the commissioners supervision then be dissolved at the end of the season, keeper contracts included. 
-	The following season the removed owners replacement will be grated their pick in the draft as well as week 1 waiver priority in order to compensate for the not having any keeper opportunities


 
2.4: Voting
 
-	Each pre-season new rules and regulations will be brought to the attention of the owners at the league draft. All rules and regulations will be voted on and must receive a majority vote to be approved. Majority vote is classified as more than 50%. 
-	A vote can be brought to the table at any point in the season if at least two managers feel the issue is worth voting on.
-	Any vote that is unanimous minus the commissioners is not subject to a veto. (Can not veto own removal vote)
-	Any vote to remove commissioner mid-season must be met with majority approval through a league vote. In such a case the co-commissioner will step up and elect a league player to take their position. 
-	When a vote is a tie the procedure is as follows: 
               Debate, Re-vote, Debate, Re-vote, COIN FLIP / Random wheel generator.
-  Any vote to change/add/remove a rule mid-season must be met with 75% approval in order to come into effect
 
2.5: Prize Money
                                        	
-	The first-place winner of the league will receive a grand prize of $855. The runner up will receive $190, and third place will receive $95.
 
2.6: Last Place Punishment
 
-	The owner who finishes in last place during the POSTSEASON will be subject to punishment. 
-	The punishment will be voted upon by the league. MUST BE AGREED UPON BEFORE THE SEASON STARTS 
-	Failure to reach an agreement will result in a commissioner appointed punishment(s).
-	2024-2025 league punishment = 12 hours at a waffle house of their choosing, where every waffle eaten by the loser results in 60 minutes off of their allotted time. The loser must provide proof of the entire completed punishment (time & waffles). 
-	2025-2026 league punishment = 12 hours at a waffle house of their choosing, where every waffle eaten by the loser results in 60 minutes off of their allotted time. The loser must provide proof of the entire completed punishment (time & waffles).

Section 4 Drafting

4.1: Draft Order

-	Draft order will be decided during the summer via NASCAR race selection

4.2: Draft Day Trades
 
-	All trades during the league draft must be written down and recorded. After the draft is completed, all trades will be processed. 
 
4.3: Draft Date, Location & Time
 
-	The draft date, location and time will be determined by the commissioner. Multiple dates and times will be allotted. Whichever date and time the majority of the owners are available, will become our official draft date. 
-	If you cannot make the draft, you will be held responsible to find someone who can draft for you. You must notify the league commissioners on who will be responsible for your draft picks. If you cannot find anyone, AUTODRAFT.
Section 5: Scoring System

-	We play in a 12 team points per reception fantasy football league.
-	The league scoring system breakdown is listed on the fantasy app. 
 
5.1: Voting on Scoring System
 
-	Each season at the draft, owners can bring forth any scoring proposals they would like to see changed. If the proposal is well received, we will proceed with a league vote. If the proposal receives a majority vote, it will be approved.
 
Section 6: Postseason

6.1: Playoffs
 
-	At the end of the season 6 teams will make the playoffs. TOP 6 TEAMS make the playoffs with the top TWO receiving a bye week the first week of the playoffs. 
 
6.2: Playoff Tiebreakers
 
-	If two teams tie in the playoffs, the playoff tiebreaker will be bench points scored. If they are also tied in bench points, it will go to a coin flip.
 
6.3: Seeding Tiebreakers
 
-	If two teams finish with the same record the tiebreakers will go as followed:
1.     Head 2 Head Record
2.    Total Points For
3.     Total Points Against

Section 7: Amendments

I.	Damar Hamlin Clause 
 In case of severe injury/death in late seasons, a game which results in being called as is, or not played, the outcome determined by the NFL is final.
II.	Marshon Sheezy Clause  
Failure to finish punishment by a set date (determined by the commissioner at the end of the season) will result in fine or removal from the league. The final decision is determined by a league vote. 
III.	Mac Clause   
Every player pick-up that exceeds the allotted amount (4) will be subject to a fine ($3).Resets after every week, no carry-overs. 
IV.	Week Winner Clause 
Each week there will be an optional pot in which owners can choose to bet on themselves to be the weekly high scorer. The owner with the highest score wins the full pot. Only owners who pitch in are eligible to receive winnings. $5 limit per owner a week. 
V.	Bet Clause 
All bets agreed upon in the group chat are final and legal tender (i.e. a bet that results in a name change…).  Functional changes not allowed, no bets for players/draft picks/etc. 
VI.	Waiver Wire Amendment
The waiver wire order is initially set after the draft and it is to be the reverse order of the draft order for that year.
VII.	Trading Amendment
The exact same trade cannot be made more than one (1) time per week. 
VIII.	Bos Clause
If it is deemed by the league (majority vote) that a team owner is intentionally tanking/self-destructing/sabotaging the integrity of the league, the commissioners have the responsibility and power to restore the integrity of the league. 


IX.	Trophy Clause
	If someone loses or damages the trophy beyond repair, than the person responsible is solely responsible for replacing the trophy back to its most recent state before the loss or damaged occurred. 
X.	Mid-Season Vote Amendment
	Any mid-season vote that involves changing/adding/removing a rule must be met with 75% (9/12) approval in order to be put into effect.
